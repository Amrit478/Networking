# Asia Engineering Site — Master Blueprint (Hard Mode Lab)

**Supernets:** `10.2.0.0/16` | `2001:DB8:2::/48`

---

### Phase 1: Physical Wiring & Topology Changes

To force Spanning Tree, EtherChannel, and routing protocols to work, your physical cables must match this precise layout:

1. **The WAN Edge Link:**
* Connect **R31 (WAN Edge)** `e0/0` to **R27 (Core L2)** `e0/0` via a single wire (configured as an 802.1Q Trunk for Router-on-a-Stick).


2. **The 4 EtherChannel Bundles (Dual-Links):**
* **R27 to R32:** Use 2 physical wires (Port-Channel 1).
* **R27 to R34:** Use 2 physical wires (Port-Channel 2).
* **R27 to R41:** Use 2 physical wires (Port-Channel 3).
* **R27 to R35:** Use 2 physical wires (Port-Channel 4).


3. **Spanning Tree Loop Cross-Connects:**
* Connect **R32** to **R33**, and **R33** to **R34**.
* Connect **R41** to **R40**, and **R40** to **R36**.
* Connect **R35** to **R39**.

### Phase 2: Layer 2 VLAN & Switch Management Setup

#### 1. The 5 Global VLANs (Modules 3, 6, & 9)

You must create these 5 VLANs on **every single switch** (`R27`, `R32`–`R41`):

* **VLAN 99:** Management
* **VLAN 100:** Development (`Dev`)
* **VLAN 101:** Testing (`Test`)
* **VLAN 102:** Quality Assurance (`QA`)
* **VLAN 103:** Guest

#### 2. Switch Management IP Addressing (Module 2)

Every switch is strictly Layer 2 and lives on VLAN 99, pointing to the router's subinterface gateway (`10.2.99.1`).

* **R27 (Core L2 Switch):** `10.2.99.2/24` (Gateway: `10.2.99.1`)
* **Access Switches (`R32` to `R41`):** Statically assigned IPs from `10.2.99.11` to `10.2.99.18` (Gateway: `10.2.99.1`).

### Phase 4: Enterprise Layer 3 Integration (OSPF & Summarization)

To tie Asia into the rest of your corporate topology (Data Center, Sales, VIP) through the central ISP/WAN cloud:

* **OSPF Area Configuration (Modules 15–17):**
* Enable OSPF on R31 pointing toward the ISP cloud in **Area 0**.
* Use the network statement `network 10.2.0.0 0.0.255.255 area 0` (or run OSPF natively on the interface).
* Ensure `passive-interface` is configured on R31's subinterfaces (`e0/0.99` through `e0/0.103`) so routing hello packets are never leaked down to the local access switches or user PCs.


* **Route Summarization (Module 19):**
* On R31, configure OSPF summary routes outward toward the WAN cloud so it advertises a single clean supernet (`10.2.0.0/16`) to the Data Center and other branch sites, keeping core routing tables optimized.
