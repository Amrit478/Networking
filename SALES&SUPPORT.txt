### Phase 1: Physical Wiring & "Hard Mode" Topology Changes

To force Spanning Tree loops, trunking, and high-availability protocols into action, modify your canvas wiring as follows:

1. **Edge-to-Core Link:**
* Keep the single wire between **R29 (WAN Edge)** and **R30 (Core L2 Switch)**. This handles 802.1Q trunking for Router-on-a-Stick.


2. **Dual-Path Distribution to Access Switches:**
* **R30 to R37:** Keep the primary link, and add a second parallel wire to form an **EtherChannel bundle (Port-Channel 1)** using LACP (`mode active`).
* **R30 to R38:** Keep the primary link, and add a second parallel wire to form an **EtherChannel bundle (Port-Channel 2)** using LACP (`mode active`).


3. **Spanning Tree Loop Cross-Connect:**
* Add a direct physical wire between **R37** and **R38**. This cross-connect creates an intentional physical loop across the distribution layer, forcing MST to actively calculate paths and block redundant ports.

### Phase 2: Layer 2 VLAN & Switch Management Setup

#### 1. The 5 Global VLANs (Modules 3, 6, & 9)

Configure these exact 5 VLANs on **every switch** (`R30`, `R37`, and `R38`):

* **VLAN 99:** Management (`Mgmt`)
* **VLAN 30:** Sales Primary (`Sales`)
* **VLAN 31:** Customer Support (`Support`)
* **VLAN 32:** Billing & Finance (`Billing`)
* **VLAN 33:** Support Partner (`Partner`)

#### 2. Switch Management IP Addressing (Module 2)

Every switch operates at Layer 2 on VLAN 99, with default gateways pointing up to the edge router:

* **R30 (Core Switch):** `10.3.99.2/24` (Gateway: `10.3.99.1`)
* **R37 (Access Switch):** `10.3.99.11/24` (Gateway: `10.3.99.1`)
* **R38 (Access Switch):** `10.3.99.12/24` (Gateway: `10.3.99.1`)

### Phase 4: Enterprise Layer 3 Integration (OSPF & Summarization)

To tie the Support & Sales site into the rest of the enterprise topology through the central ISP/WAN cloud:

* **OSPF Area Configuration (Modules 15–17):**
* Enable OSPF on **R29** pointing toward the WAN cloud in **Area 0**.
* Use the network statement `network 10.3.0.0 0.0.255.255 area 0`.
* Ensure `passive-interface` is enabled on all subinterfaces (`e0/3.99` through `e0/3.33`) to prevent OSPF hello chatter from reaching client access ports.


* **Route Summarization (Module 19):**
* Configure OSPF summary routes on **R29** to advertise a clean aggregate supernet (`10.3.0.0/16`) to the rest of the enterprise network.
