## 1. Scenario Overview: Apex Robotics Global Network

**Apex Robotics** operates across five global locations connected via a core BGP WAN backbone. The company builds automated robotics systems, requiring strict isolation between administrative offices, production floors, IoT infrastructure, and data centers.

### The 5 Locations & Roles

* **Data Center 1 (NA - North America):** Primary HQ & Cloud Services
* **Data Center 2 (EU - Europe):** Failover DC & European Operations
* **Engineering Site (Asia):** R&D, Firmware Testing, Dev Sandboxes
* **VIP / Executive Site (NA):** C-Suite, Sensitive Operations, High Security
* **Support & Sales Site (NA):** Customer Support, Call Center, Sales Teams

## 4. Key Security Policies & Traffic Routing Rules

1. **Industrial Network Isolation (VLAN 101, 102, 103):**
* **Rule:** Robot Controllers and PLCs are strictly restricted to **East-West Traffic**.
* **Enforcement:** No internet access (`0.0.0.0/0`) permitted. Communication allowed only between PLC Controllers, Quality Inspection, and Manufacturing Database.


2. **DMZ Support Network (VLAN 107):**
* **Rule:** Third-party vendor support can access `10.60.10.128/27` via encrypted SSL-VPN.
* **Enforcement:** Firewall policies restrict outbound access from the DMZ into internal production networks without explicit multi-factor authentication.


3. **Global WAN Routing:**
* **Protocol:** eBGP configured between WAN edge devices (`CSR1000v` / `FortiGates`) and the simulated ISP core.
* **Inter-DC Traffic:** Encrypted IPsec tunnels established over WAN transit links (`10.10.99.0/24` to `10.20.99.0/24`).
