### Step 4: Unified OSPF Routing Configuration

To make every site fully reachable across the entire enterprise topology, run **OSPF Area 0** across the central ISP cloud and all site edge routers.

1. **On `ISP-Core1` and `ISP-Core2`:**

```text

router ospf 1

 router-id 1.1.1.1 (or 2.2.2.2)

 network 10.255.0.0 0.0.255.255 area 0

```

2. **On Site Edge Routers (`R4`, `R5`, `R31`, `R29`, `R42`):**

* Ensure that in addition to their internal network statements, they also participate in OSPF over their respective WAN interfaces pointing to the ISP cloud.

* Because you configured route summarization on each edge router earlier, **each site will cleanly inject only its aggregate supernet** (`10.1.0.0/16`, `10.2.0.0/16`, `10.3.0.0/16`, and `10.4.0.0/16`) into the central OSPF domain, keeping routing tables optimized across the entire enterprise.
