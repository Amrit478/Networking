## STATIC vs DHCP — Summary

**Fully Static (no DHCP anywhere in the site):**
- Data Center — every device (firewall links, core SVIs, switch mgmt IPs, all Linux servers)
- ISP/WAN Cloud — every link (backbone + all 5 site-to-ISP links)

**Static:**
- All switch/router management interfaces on VLAN 99, across every site (Asia, Support & Sales, VIP)
- All router subinterfaces acting as gateways (the `.1` address on every VLAN)
- All point-to-point WAN/firewall links everywhere

**DHCP (client-assigned only):**
- Every VPC endpoint in Asia Engineering, Support & Sales, and VIP Executive — pulling from their respective router's DHCP pools (`.50`–`.200` range on each VLAN subinterface)

**Rule of thumb across this whole design:** infrastructure devices (routers, switches, firewalls) are always static; end-user endpoints (VPCs) are always DHCP, except in the Data Center, where even the Linux servers are static since that site is explicitly "100% static."
