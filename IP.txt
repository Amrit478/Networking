## 1. Data Center (Site 1) — 100% Static Addressing

**Supernets:** `10.1.0.0/16` | `2001:DB8:1::/48`

In the Data Center, everything is static. Since you have two Core L3 Routers/Switches (`R4` and `R5`), they act as redundant gateways for your Linux servers using HSRP/VRRP. The Virtual IP is `.1`, `R4` is `.2`, and `R5` is `.3`.

### Point-to-Point Links (Core Routers to Firewall)

| Node | Interface | Connection | IPv4 Address (/30) | IPv6 Address (/127) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **R4 (Core 1)** | `e0/x` | To Firewall | `10.1.255.1` | `2001:DB8:1:255::1` |
| **Firewall** | `port2` | To R4 | `10.1.255.2` | `2001:DB8:1:255::2` |
| **R5 (Core 2)** | `e0/y` | To Firewall | `10.1.255.5` | `2001:DB8:1:255::4` |
| **Firewall** | `port3` | To R5 | `10.1.255.6` | `2001:DB8:1:255::5` |

### Core Router SVIs (Default Gateways for Linux & Switches)

| VLAN & Purpose | R4 (Core 1) IP | R5 (Core 2) IP | HSRP Virtual IP (Gateway) | IPv6 Virtual Gateway (/64) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **VLAN 10 (Web/App)** | `10.1.10.2/24` | `10.1.10.3/24` | **`10.1.10.1`** | `2001:DB8:1:10::1` |
| **VLAN 20 (DB/Mon)** | `10.1.20.2/24` | `10.1.20.3/24` | **`10.1.20.1`** | `2001:DB8:1:20::1` |
| **VLAN 99 (Mgmt)** | `10.1.99.2/24` | `10.1.99.3/24` | **`10.1.99.1`** | `2001:DB8:1:99::1` |

## 4. VIP Executive (Site 4) — Dynamic/DHCP Edge

**Supernets:** `10.4.0.0/16` | `2001:DB8:4::/48`

This site consists of 1 Firewall, 1 Edge Router, 1 Access Switch, and 4 VPCs. Because there is only one router, you will use **Router-on-a-Stick (Module 8)** to handle the VLAN gateways using Subinterfaces (e.g., `e0/1.40`).
